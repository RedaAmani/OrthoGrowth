OrthoGrowth

Local lower-limb length discrepancy estimator

Calculation prototype, not a validated medical device. Do not use alone to decide treatment or epiphysiodesis. Measurements, assumptions and results require specialist review.

Patient and measurement date

The published article suggests considering bone age after 10 years, depending on pubertal stage and clinical context. The selected age basis should be clinically justified.

Bilateral segment measurements

Calculations

The congenital model applies the multiplier to each segment. The developmental model requires comparable previous measurements. The three epiphysiodesis estimates are calculated separately.

The congenital model uses Δm = Δ × M. The developmental model estimates Δm = Δ + i × G from serial measurements. The three theoretical epiphysiodesis estimates are shown separately: distal femur, proximal tibia and combined. They use published constants (0.71, 0.57 and 0.67) and require clinical verification.
