<p><strong>OrthoGrowth</strong></p><p>Estimates of lower-limb length discrepancy and theoretical epiphysiodesis timing in pediatric orthopedics, using the multiplier method. English and French interface.</p><blockquote><p><em>Clinical disclaimer. OrthoGrowth is a calculation prototype, not a clinically validated medical device. Its calculations, assumptions and multiplier data require independent verification. Do not use it as the sole basis for diagnosis, treatment planning or epiphysiodesis decisions. Verify all measurements and results with a qualified specialist.</em></p></blockquote><p>Test with fictitious data before any real use.</p><p>Download it from the <a href="https://github.com/RedaAmani/OrthoGrowth/releases">Releases</a> page.</p><p><strong>Sources and attribution</strong></p><p>The multiplier-method approach is based on:</p><p>- Paley D, Bhave A, Herzenberg JE, Bowen JR.
